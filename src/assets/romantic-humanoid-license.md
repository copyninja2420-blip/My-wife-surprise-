# Romantic humanoid

Source: Grab3D, Man Rigged, https://grab3d.com/dl/man-rigged/man-rigged.glb

License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain, https://grab3d.com/license/

Modified: reduced mesh complexity and texture resolution; recolored clothing, skin, and hair. This is an approximate companion, not a photo-derived custom head or exact likeness.

Photo-inspired variant: the same CC0 skinned mesh, with the user-uploaded portrait transferred into its face texture, fuller swept-up hair vertex shaping, and pink centre fabric. The body and clothing silhouette remain an approximation rather than a custom photogrammetry scan or bespoke shirt mesh.
